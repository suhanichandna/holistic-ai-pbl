HOLISTIC AI

Intelligent multi-domain human performance tracking and guidance system.

Problem Statement

Modern students and professionals struggle to track and optimize performance across multiple life domains such as physical health, cognitive work, emotional stability, and lifestyle habits. Existing tools are fragmented and do not provide integrated insights.

System Methodology

🗄️

Dataset / Input

User-logged daily performance metrics across physical, cognitive, emotional, creative, skills, and lifestyle domains.

🧠

Model / Architecture

Modular dashboard architecture with domain-wise analytics, behavioral summaries, and AI-driven guidance engine.

📈

Model Performance

Comparative performance analysis of Holistic AI vs traditional systems.

🔗 View Working Demo

Results & Analysis

The Holistic AI system successfully enables structured performance tracking across multiple domains. Users receive real-time analytics, historical insights, and AI-based recommendations for optimization.
